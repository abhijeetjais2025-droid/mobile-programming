THE PROBLEM

Parking Shouldn't Be This Difficult.

Finding a suitable parking space is a major challenge for vehicle owners in busy areas of Kathmandu Valley. Drivers often spend considerable time searching for available parking.

This contributes to traffic congestion, unnecessary fuel consumption, and frustration. There is also limited real-time information about parking availability, prices, and locations.
